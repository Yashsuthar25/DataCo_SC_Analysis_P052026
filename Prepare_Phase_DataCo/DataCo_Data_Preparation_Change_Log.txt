DataCo Logistics Performance Analysis
Data Preparation – Change Log

Purpose
-------
The original DataCo Supply Chain dataset contains information covering customers,
products, sales, logistics, order fulfillment, and other business areas.

For this project, I am focusing on logistics and order-fulfillment performance,
with product categories and geographic information used to understand differences
in delivery performance.

I kept the original CSV file unchanged and created a separate working version.
The following columns were removed from the working dataset because they were
not required for the current business task, contained sensitive/personal
information, or did not provide useful variation.

Removed Columns & Reasons
-------------------------

1. Email
   Customer contact information is not required for the logistics analysis.

2. Customer Fname
   Personal customer information is not relevant to the business questions.

3. Customer Lname
   Personal customer information is not relevant to the business questions.

4. Customer Password
   Sensitive customer information and completely unrelated to the analysis.

5. Latitude
   Detailed coordinate information is not required for the current project scope.

6. Longitude
   Detailed coordinate information is not required for the current project scope.

7. Product Image
   Image/reference information does not contribute to the logistics analysis.

8. Product Status
   This field contains only 0s in the dataset, so it does not provide useful
   variation for analysis.

9. Product Price
   Product pricing is outside the current logistics-focused business task.

10. Order Item Product Price
    Product pricing is outside the current logistics-focused business task.

11. Order Item Discount
    Discount analysis is outside the current project scope.

12. Order Item Discount Rate
    Discount analysis is outside the current project scope.

13. Sales per customer
    Customer-level sales analysis is outside the current project scope.

14. Order Item Profit Ratio
    Profitability analysis is outside the current project scope.

15. Order Profit Per Order
    Profitability analysis is outside the current project scope.

Preparation Note
----------------
The purpose of removing these fields was not to remove as much data as possible,
but to create a focused working dataset containing the information needed to
answer the defined business questions.

The original CSV has been retained separately as the raw/master dataset. If a
removed field becomes relevant later in the analysis, it can be restored from
the original file.
